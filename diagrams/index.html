#dependency-graph

What the six domain pipelines converge on

GOLD 4A (Dependency Graph · Interactive Step Mode): Ranked by dependency depth. The orchestrator reads one routing table and triggers six domain pipelines; those six deploy into the product repositories and converge on two shared projects. The dashed line is the one that is not a tree: a merged MR arrives back at the orchestrator as a webhook event, which is what the anti-loop guard exists to stop.
